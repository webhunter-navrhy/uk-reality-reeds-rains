About the property

Description property

This Harron Homes property is located on the outskirts of Chesterfield town centre and is conveniently placed for all amenities. Sainsburys supermarket is a stone throws away and there are additional amenities in the nearby areas of Brimington and Whittington Moor including doctors and dentists. Transport links are excellent and the M1 motorway is within easy access. On the development there is a park which is perfect for children on evenings and weekends and the canal is within walking distance which provides lovely weekend walks.

The property briefly comprises of a entrance hallway, living room, dining kitchen, utility room and downstairs WC. To the first floor are four double bedrooms (master with en-suite) and a family bathroom. Gardens to front and rear. Driveway providing off road parking for two vehicles and leading to a integrated single garage.
